*16 October 2026*

**We are thrilled that Sedos has been invited to return to Cornwall’s spectacular outdoor theatre, the Minack, in 2028.**

Our debut at the Minack was in 2019 with *[A Swell Party](https://www.sedos.co.uk/shows/2019-a-swell-party)*, a tribute to the life and work of Cole Porter, and in 2025, we followed up with a sold-out run of *[The Pirate Queen](https://www.sedos.co.uk/shows/2025-the-pirate-queen)*, a lesser-known gem from the writers of *Les Misérables* and *Miss Saigon*.

The opportunity to take another show to the Minack is incredibly exciting for our membership, both on and off stage – performing and working in this beautiful outdoor theatre with the sea as its backdrop is unique and the experience of spending a week in Cornwall with all your show mates is very special. 

We’ve learnt a huge amount from our two previous shows at the Minack and look forward to building on that with our 2028 production.

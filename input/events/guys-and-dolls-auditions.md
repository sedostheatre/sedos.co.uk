**Information is now available about *[Guys And Dolls](https://www.sedos.co.uk/shows/2027-guys-and-dolls)* auditions, so you can find out how to try out for Sedos’ first musical of 2027.** 

*Guys And Dolls* plays at the [Bridewell Theatre](https://www.sedos.co.uk/venues/bridewell) from 5-15 May 2027.

**KEY INFO**

**Pre-audition workshop:** two sessions (7pm and 8.30pm) on Tuesday 10 November\
**First round in person auditions:** in person dance call on 14 November or 17 November, and self tape - deadline Tuesday 1 December 10am\
**Recalls (by invitation):** Sunday 13 December
